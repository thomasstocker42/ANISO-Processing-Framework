# R2STAR03 — GRE R2* fit-method validation

**Status:** Finalized
**Implementation:** R2STAR03 v0.2.0
**Production dependency:** R2STAR02 v0.1.0

## Purpose

R2STAR03 evaluates whether the log-linear fitting strategy adopted in
R2STAR02 introduces a practically relevant bias relative to direct nonlinear
least-squares fitting of the original GRE magnitude signal.

R2STAR03 is a validation module only. It does not replace or modify the
production R2* reconstruction generated by R2STAR02.

## Signal model

Both methods use the same mono-exponential GRE magnitude model,

\[
S(TE)=S_0\exp(-R_2^*TE).
\]

The production R2STAR02 implementation minimizes residuals after logarithmic
transformation,

\[
\ln S(TE)=\ln S_0-R_2^*TE,
\]

using ordinary unweighted least squares.

The validation comparator instead minimizes residuals directly in the
original magnitude domain,

\[
\min_{S_0,R_2^*}
\sum_i
\left[
S_i-S_0\exp(-R_2^*TE_i)
\right]^2.
\]

The direct fit is solved with `scipy.optimize.least_squares`.

## Echo selection

All 12 GRE echoes are retained for both fitting approaches.

This differs from the first-echo-excluded strategy selected for the
multi-refocusing MC-SE R2 acquisition. The GRE acquisition does not contain
the repeated refocusing-pulse train responsible for stimulated-echo and
coherence-pathway effects in MC-SE data. Retaining the first GRE echo also
preserves the high-SNR early portion of the T2* decay.

Using an identical echo set in R2STAR02 and R2STAR03 ensures that the
validation isolates the effect of fit domain rather than simultaneously
testing echo selection.

## Validation sampling

Validation was performed across all 10 in-situ cases.

For each case, 10,000 voxels were sampled deterministically from the
R2STAR02 valid-fit support. Sampling was stratified across deciles of the
R2STAR02 R2* distribution using a fixed random seed (`20260909`).

The stratification ensures coverage of the full fitted R2* range.
Consequently, global metrics across the sampled voxels should not be
interpreted as population-weighted whole-brain statistics.

## Direct-fit constraints

The direct nonlinear fit used:

- the same 12 metadata-derived echo times as R2STAR02;
- the same R2STAR02 valid-fit support;
- finite, strictly positive magnitude samples;
- at least six valid echoes;
- initial R2* and S0 values from R2STAR02;
- R2* bounds of \(10^{-6}\) to \(1000~\mathrm{s^{-1}}\);
- positive S0;
- trust-region reflective least-squares optimization;
- maximum 200 function evaluations.

## Fit-quality analyses

Agreement was evaluated globally and after stratification according to fit
quality.

The following diagnostics were calculated:

- quartiles of the R2STAR02 log-domain RMSE;
- quartiles of the direct-fit magnitude-domain NRMSE;
- exploratory subsets defined by log-RMSE thresholds of 0.05, 0.10 and 0.20;
- exploratory direct-fit NRMSE thresholds of 0.10 and 0.20.

For each subset, the following agreement metrics were reported:

- median direct-minus-log-linear R2* difference;
- mean absolute error;
- root-mean-square error;
- Pearson correlation;
- median absolute difference;
- 95th percentile absolute difference;
- direct-on-log-linear regression slope and intercept.

The fit-quality thresholds are validation diagnostics only and are not used
as production exclusion criteria.

## Production decision

R2STAR03 demonstrated that the production log-linear estimator agrees closely
with direct magnitude-domain nonlinear fitting when the mono-exponential model
describes the GRE signal adequately.

Large method disagreements were concentrated in voxels with poor
mono-exponential fit quality rather than reflecting a cohort-wide estimator
bias.

R2STAR02 therefore remains the production R2* reconstruction.
